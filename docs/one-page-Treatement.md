# IT265 Module 2: One-Page Treatment

**Working game title:** Embers

Write one coherent, one-page-length treatment. Use the four prompts to begin, then combine your answers into natural prose.

## Four-Sentence Core

1. **Premise:** The player is the major or leader of the last civilization and is in charge on managing it, you have to decide what you sent out your units to do, balancing risk and reward while managing the survival of your town.
2. **Experience and loop:** The loop involves the player constantly sending out parties into the wilderness to gather supplies, while fighting encounters, and using the supplies to upgrade the town or the characters to challenge bigger dungeons. 
3. **Goal and pressure:** The goal is for the player to explore the post apocalyptic world and discover the secrets behind it, perhaps even defeating the remanence of the apocalyps.
4. **Scope:** I wish to test how the loop between the characters and the cycle will play out, so I think I would create a party and a sample game loop. 

## Treatment Draft

My game is a party builder, dungeon crawler, turn based, base building game that is set in a post apocalyptic world that. 
You play as the leader of the last town still holding out against the wild apocalypse, sending out parties into the apocalypse to find supplies to upgrade your heros or town.  
The player has to balance the risk of challenging dangerous dungeon for better supplies but risk the death of your parties or taking safer explorations and risking the apocalypse taking over the town. 
The player's goal is for exploration to open up more of the map and story all the while the exploration will encounter harder enemies. 
I want to first set up a sample cycles of the loop to see if it will even work.
