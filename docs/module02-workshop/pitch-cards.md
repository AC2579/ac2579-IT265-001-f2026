# IT265 Module 2: Concept Pitch Cards

Copy this card for each of your two or three shortlisted concepts. Aim to explain each in about one minute. Use your [idea bank](./01-idea-bank.md) as a starting point.

## Pitch Card

**Working title:** Embers

**Player role and situation:** In charge of the last knows civilization, the player must make decisions to survive and explore the world.

**Repeated decision or action:** Upgrading building and characters to send them out for expeditions, on which they might encounter combat or events.

**Goal, pressure, or ending:** The goal is for the player to progress their character and town enough to encounter the apocalyps.

**Hook:** "The world is at an end, you mush lead what is left of humanity to survive the apocalypse, so form your team of adventures and warriors, and strike out against the apocalypse, for good or worst your decisions will lead us to death or damnation.   

**Smallest useful physical prototype:** A prototype dungeon and combat system, before figuring out the base building system.

**Question I want listeners to answer:** Should the combat be challenging? Would you play a table top dungeon crawler? 

Keep the cards as working notes. The workshop does not add a separate graded pitch submission.
